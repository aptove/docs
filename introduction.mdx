## What is Aptove?

Aptove is an open-source mobile client for AI agents built on the [Agent Client Protocol (ACP)](https://github.com/agent-client-protocol). It consists of three main components:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bridge" icon="bridge" href="/bridge/overview">
    A local Rust binary that runs on your machine and exposes any ACP-compatible agent over WebSocket. Supports multiple transports so you can connect from anywhere.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile App" icon="mobile" href="/mobile-app/overview">
    Native iOS and Android apps that connect to the bridge and let you chat with agents from your phone.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it works

```
Mobile App  ──[transport]──  Bridge  ──[stdio]──  AI Agent
```

1. You run the **bridge** on the same machine as your AI agent.
2. You scan a QR code in the bridge TUI to pair your phone.
3. The **mobile app** opens a WebSocket connection through whichever transport you chose (local or Tailscale).
4. Messages flow bidirectionally over ACP — the bridge pipes them to your agent's stdin/stdout.

## Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the bridge">
    Download the latest release from [GitHub](https://github.com/aptove/bridge/releases) or build from source with `cargo build --release`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the bridge">
    Run `bridge` in your terminal. The setup wizard walks you through choosing a transport and an agent command.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair your phone">
    The bridge displays a QR code. Open the Aptove app, tap **Add Agent**, and scan it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Send your first message. The bridge launches your agent and relays responses back to your phone in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
