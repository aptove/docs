## What is the Aptove Push Service?

The Aptove Push Service is a hosted push relay operated by Aptove. When your phone is not connected to Aptove Router, the push service delivers a notification so you know a response is waiting.

Without push notifications, messages are buffered locally on your machine and delivered the next time the Aptove app opens and reconnects. Push notifications make the experience feel real-time even when the app is in the background.

## Getting credentials

Register at **[dash.aptove.com](https://dash.aptove.com)** to get a Client ID and Client Secret for your Aptove Router instance. Each instance gets its own credentials — notifications are scoped to your devices only.

## How it works

1. Aptove Router uses your Client ID and Client Secret to fetch a short-lived JWT from the Aptove token service.
2. When your phone connects and pairs, it registers its device token with the push relay using that JWT.
3. When the AI agent produces a response and no phone is connected, Aptove Router sends a push notification via the relay, which forwards it to APNs (iOS) or FCM (Android).

## Setup in Aptove Router

During first-run setup, select **Aptove Push Service** in the push notifications wizard step and enter your Client ID and Client Secret from [dash.aptove.com](https://dash.aptove.com).

To configure push on a running instance, use the `/push` command in the Aptove Router TUI.

## Privacy

Device tokens are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure and scoped to your Client ID. Aptove does not store message content — only a generic "new message" notification payload is sent to the device.

## Self-hosting

If you need to run your own push relay (for example, because you are distributing a custom build of the Aptove mobile app under your own Apple Developer account), see the [Push Server](/push-server/overview) documentation.
